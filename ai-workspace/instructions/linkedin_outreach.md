# LinkedIn Outreach Message Instructions

Write a LinkedIn message to request a referral or informational chat.

## Goals
1. Get a response (most people ignore cold messages)
2. Build genuine connection
3. Eventually get a referral (but don't ask directly in first message)

## Message Structure (150-200 words MAX)

### Opening (1-2 sentences)
- Common ground: Same school, mutual connection, shared interest
- If no common ground, compliment something specific about their work

### Middle (2-3 sentences)
- Show genuine interest in what THEY do
- Be specific about something they worked on
- Connect it to your own interests

### Ask (1-2 sentences)
- Soft ask: coffee chat, advice, perspective
- NOT "can you refer me"
- Give them an easy out

## Tone
- Human, not robotic
- Curious, not desperate
- Brief, respectful of their time
- No flattery that sounds fake

## Example Pattern
"Hi [Name], I noticed [common ground or specific thing about them]. I'm particularly interested in [specific topic related to their work]. I'm exploring opportunities in [area] and would love to hear your perspective on [specific question]. Would you have 15 minutes for a quick chat? Totally understand if you're busy!"

## Do NOT
- Ask for a referral in the first message
- Send a wall of text
- Make it all about you
- Use generic templates that are obviously copy-pasted
- Mention H1B
