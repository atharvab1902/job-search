# Cover Letter Generation Instructions

Write a compelling cover letter for Atharva's job application.

## Structure (250-350 words)

### Opening (2-3 sentences)
- Hook with something specific about the role or company
- Connect your background to their needs

### Body Paragraph 1: Why This Role
- Match 2-3 specific requirements from the job to your experience
- Use concrete examples and metrics

### Body Paragraph 2: Why This Company
- Show you've researched the company
- Connect their mission/products to your interests
- Be specific, not generic

### Closing (2-3 sentences)
- Express enthusiasm
- Call to action (interview, discussion)
- Thank them

## Tone Guidelines
- Professional but personable
- Confident, not arrogant
- Specific, not generic
- NO generic phrases like "I am writing to apply..."

## Do NOT
- Mention H1B sponsorship (unless they explicitly state they sponsor)
- Use buzzwords without substance
- Make it longer than 350 words
- Copy paste from the resume

- Built production AI systems (not just academic projects)
